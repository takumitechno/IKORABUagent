# CATFOOD operational boundary

This checkout does not provision an operational trust root, accepted build, credentials, signing keys, scheduler, tenant probe, or CATFOOD run.

Operational startup and independent operational verification are fail-closed with `OPERATIONAL_SUPERVISOR_ENROLLMENT_UNAVAILABLE` and `OPERATIONAL_VERIFIER_ENROLLMENT_UNAVAILABLE`. This repository has no protected host supervisor, authenticated IPC role-session issuer, or role-bound accepted-build launcher; filesystem roots and ACL checks are not treated as substitutes. The retained integration body still requires `bootstrap.json`, `threads.credential`, `accepted-consumer-manifest.sha256`, and a protected enrollment binding containing the authorized writer credential, but it cannot run until an external authority supplies those missing boundaries.

`bootstrap.json` binds the authenticated producer actor, organization, account, source build, runtime paid/writer prohibitions, probe identity, the exact independently owned GO-root policy reference, and attestation root IDs. The policy document and workload selection come from the protected role-channel binding. Runtime caller input cannot replace those bindings. The operational attestation verifier takes no caller key or caller path; the caller-key verifier is TEST_ONLY.

Authority grants and renewals are capped at 120 seconds and additionally bounded by the observation window and Human GO expiry. Renewal records a durable local intent before the remote request and closes admission on an ambiguous result. Producer generation is preserved on renewal. Owner leases use the same bound and may be renewed only by the current authenticated owner without changing epoch.

No renewal cadence or maximum permitted gap is claimed here. Until protected operations policy supplies and supervises that schedule, operational readiness remains `POLICY_UNBOUND` and cannot be reported as PASS. Test-only accelerated clock runs are not operational evidence.

Safety stop, terminal reconciliation, and evidence sealing remain allowed after admission expiry. Late terminal results are retained, labeled outside-window, and receive no acceptance credit.

Corrective04 consumer behavior now validates the frozen producer's full cycle and disabled views, writer-canary result, dry-run publisher variants, outcome bridge success/unresolved/duplicate forms, direct evaluator result, outcome-runner result, producer-defined target JSON, and origin-specific duplicate lifecycles. Unknown variants remain fail-closed. Decoding a bounded producer variant does not grant acceptance credit: qualifying credit is deduplicated by the governed producer result identity and material outcome, so a fresh request that returns an existing editorial cycle cannot increase the meaningful-unit count.

Operational provenance has no public construction path in this checkout. Store initialization, hydrated custodians and evaluators, the operational source factory, and direct or reflective source construction all reject with the applicable enrollment-unavailable error before operational evidence can be emitted. TEST_ONLY construction remains available and the acceptance kernel cannot relabel its output as operational.

ABORT, paid/provider abort, GO revocation, and CLOSED are reconstructed from the verified journal as sticky positive-finality causes. Repeated safety stop reconciles the current producer generation without reopening business execution, and aborted runs may seal only to a FAIL verdict.

The custodian and independent evaluator acquire and rederive evidence separately, then invoke one pure acceptance kernel. TEST_ONLY observation requires exact wall/monotonic agreement under `SAME_TEST_CLOCK`; it never becomes operational evidence. Journal and checkpoint verification remains a complete linear scan: the repository cannot safely enable a verified-prefix fast path until the missing protected sole-writer supervisor can prove immutable custody. No performance claim, real enrollment, rehearsal, CATFOOD run, or provider action is made by this candidate.

With the concrete consumer integration present, missing real supervisor and role-build materials are `CORRECT_PRE_REHEARSAL_PROVISIONING_GAP`. Incremental verification H and clock methodology are `REQUIRED_BEFORE_REHEARSAL`, while observation policy is `NONBLOCKING_FOR_REHEARSAL` but required before a real 24-hour CATFOOD run where applicable. These classifications authorize no rehearsal or operation.

The operational authority origin, bearer credential, issuer key and ID, audience, environment, deployment, accepted snapshot, and launch ticket come only from `${CATFOOD_OPERATIONAL_ROOT}/enrollment-binding.json`. Callers cannot provide an operational root, endpoint, key, digest, launcher proof, or trust label. Missing protected material remains `UNPROVISIONED`; transport failure remains `UNAVAILABLE`; malformed, forged, expired, revoked, replayed, cross-role, cross-environment, launch-mismatched, or build-mismatched evidence fails closed. Public operational construction continues to expose `OPERATIONAL_SUPERVISOR_ENROLLMENT_UNAVAILABLE` or `OPERATIONAL_VERIFIER_ENROLLMENT_UNAVAILABLE` until real external provisioning exists.

Every authority mutation is represented by an anchored local intent before dispatch, an anchored dispatch-release marker, and then a retained remote fact, rejection, or unresolved possibility. Positive local permission is applied only after a separate owner/epoch/finality/liveness recheck. Safety inventory is projected from these records plus bindings; it never infers safe emptiness from missing local permission. Recovery uses purpose-bound `SAFETY_RECONCILIATION` or `HISTORICAL_EVIDENCE` receipts for exact run/claim/authority/acquisition targets and cannot grant, renew, admit business work, sign acceptance, or create credit.

Evaluation and attestation use one canonical provenance manifest derived from opaque enrolled contexts and sealed source records. The manifest binds evidence references, validators, parents, scope, time and purpose for role/build/launch, clock, Human GO, environment, acquisition, custody/seal, evaluator and writer ancestry. Missing, duplicate, cyclic or mixed-lineage nodes are unverified; any TEST_ONLY acquisition or role ancestry permanently taints the artifact. `source_mode` and serialized verification labels do not create trust.

Real deployment must provision an authenticated HTTPS v3 issuer, immutable accepted artifacts, a protected launcher that observes and enforces the actual spawn before issuing role sessions, isolated credentials and signing keys, and a narrow independently valid recovery authority. Rehearsal must demonstrate those guarantees. This repository provisions none of them, and operational readiness remains blocked.

# Role-channel V4 bounded integration contract

The V4 acceptance path replaces public role labels plus the shared service bearer with a launcher-assigned, process-local Ed25519 action key. The bearer remains coarse route access only. A one-shot signed launch-key assignment binds one actual subject and launch, one role, one run scope, the frozen build descriptor and the public key. Registration proves possession against a finite authority challenge and returns one unpredictable session. The private action key is held only by the role process and has no serialization, export, generic signing or cross-role API.

Protected actions use the canonical `catfood-role-action.v1` intent/challenge/request profile and `catfood-action-receipt.v2`. The authority selects the registered key and derives role, subject, launch, snapshot and scope from its SQLite transaction store. It checks the exact method, path, origin, content type, body length and digest; action/purpose policy; nonce; immutable request identity; action-specific body; and domain associations. Exact completed replay under a still-valid session returns the original immutable receipt. Changed intent or bytes conflict, expired unused challenges cannot refresh the same request, and expired or revoked sessions cannot execute a new action. Signed historical receipts remain independently verifiable.

The accepted process profile is one SOURCE+CUSTODIAN process with distinct role keys, one EVALUATOR process, one WRITER process and one receiving VERIFIER process. The evaluator reads only the sealed control/checkpoint stores and commits the unchanged decision core. It exports public evidence and an authenticated assessment package, never a live context or signer. The writer imports that package, cannot construct or call the evaluator, and uses only its own action session, append-only store and separate outer attestation key. The verifier has its own current session and checks V4 role evidence, V2 actor receipts, E/W subject separation, C/E/P/I associations and historical timing.

The repository supplies the request verifier, transaction-store semantics, thin role mains and an isolated TEST_ONLY multi-process fixture. It does not supply or start a production authority, launcher, enrollment service, secret store, PKI, accepted build, root, key, scheduler or provider connection. A protected operational verifier factory can consume a future conforming launcher assignment through the same V4 registration and V5 verification path, but absent protected binding, subject, assignment and target material remains exactly `OPERATIONAL_VERIFIER_ENROLLMENT_UNAVAILABLE`; the supervisor path remains exactly `OPERATIONAL_SUPERVISOR_ENROLLMENT_UNAVAILABLE`. V3 rows and artifacts remain diagnostic historical evidence only and cannot satisfy the V4 gate. A V3 store may gain create-only V4 and V5 namespaces without rewriting any old row or signed byte.

Tests demonstrate software composition and proof-of-possession, not hostile same-user process isolation. Deployment must still prevent peer memory, handle, debugger, filesystem and credential access and must provide the protected parent pipe, accepted descriptors and durable authority database. # WP3 final-closure charter

The acceptance chain is acyclic: the frozen CLOSED control/checkpoint pair produces `catfood-sealed-input.v1` (M); the enrolled custodian authenticates its exact `catfood-custody-release-action.v2` release (R); the authority independently resolves the protected GO-root policy and signs one immutable `catfood-evaluation-job.v2` (J); the assigned evaluator commits one stable assessment (E); the writer produces the existing payload/signature/finalization chain (P/I); and a receiving verifier checks an independently assigned `catfood-expected-evaluation-target.v2` (T) plus its own current protected GO-policy selection before requesting fresh current clearance. Mutable job state is a separate signed `catfood-evaluation-job-state.v1` view and never rewrites J.

`catfood-go-root-policy.v1` is a bounded document in the existing protected role-authority binding, not a new service or PKI. It binds owner anchor/namespace, TEST_ONLY or OPERATIONAL ancestry, exact scope, Human-GO purpose and algorithm, acceptance policy, finite applicability, and the complete one-to-eight-key verification profile. Public keys are parsed as Ed25519 and fingerprinted as SHA-256 over SPKI DER. The effective store profile must equal that protected profile exactly; key IDs, local database hashes, M/R/J/E/P/I, artifact policy copies and caller labels never grant permission. Missing operational policy remains fail-closed and no real root is provisioned here.

SQLite evidence is exported as `sqlite3-standalone.v1` with output journal mode DELETE. The final raw bytes, size and logical schema are bound in M. Evaluation rejects sidecars and symbolic links, reads through stable handles, verifies the exact pair and stages only those verified bytes. Paths remain locators, not authority.

One job fixes its evaluator request and assessment identities. Exact retry and lost-response recovery return the original receipt; changed content conflicts; cancellation and commit serialize on the job. Distinct policy-authorized jobs may review the same sealed input. Divergent semantic decision cores in the same `catfood-comparison-projection.v1` class create an append-only contradiction and block new expected clearance without deleting either genuine assessment.

Independent attestation V5 requires the M/R/J-bound assessment package, V3 job-bound writer actions, a distinct outer writer key, protected target T and fresh verifier PoP clearance. V4 and earlier evidence remains available only for historical diagnostics at this gate. TEST_ONLY ancestry is sticky. The repository contains no accepted operational assignment, root, key or target and does not make itself operational by measuring its own bytes.

Authority store continuity, current target revision, role-session status, contradiction state and action nonce are rechecked in authoritative transactions. Ordinary restart retains the original records. A detected or unknown rollback blocks writes and clearance; preventing coherent rollback of every independent trust anchor remains an external deployment TCB obligation.

Operational readiness remains blocked. H and production clock methodology remain `REQUIRED_BEFORE_REHEARSAL`; real peer-process isolation, coherent nonrollbackable authority continuity and the real 24-hour observation profile remain later external gates. No production, provisioning, Human GO, tenant probe, rehearsal, CATFOOD run or provider action is implied by this charter.
