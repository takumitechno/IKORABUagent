# Corrective07 controlled launch and recovery contract

Operational role construction uses the v3 consumer protocol in [catfood-enrollment-v3.json](contracts/catfood-enrollment-v3.json). The signed response must carry an independently authenticated launcher-observed record for the realized role process. That record binds the accepted snapshot and role descriptor to the actual main, role exports, Bun executable and version, argv/execArgv, loading arguments, relevant loading-environment hashes, cwd, resolved bunfig/tsconfig/package configuration, subject/session, artifact root, and an externally enforced lifetime-immutability policy. Local measurement is only a consistency check. Enrollment never creates the launcher record, and v1/v2 records cannot satisfy v3.

The operational authority origin, bearer credential, issuer key and ID, audience, environment, deployment, accepted snapshot, and launch ticket come only from `${CATFOOD_OPERATIONAL_ROOT}/enrollment-binding.json`. Callers cannot provide an operational root, endpoint, key, digest, launcher proof, or trust label. Missing protected material remains `UNPROVISIONED`; transport failure remains `UNAVAILABLE`; malformed, forged, expired, revoked, replayed, cross-role, cross-environment, launch-mismatched, or build-mismatched evidence fails closed. Public operational construction continues to expose `OPERATIONAL_SUPERVISOR_ENROLLMENT_UNAVAILABLE` or `OPERATIONAL_VERIFIER_ENROLLMENT_UNAVAILABLE` until real external provisioning exists.

Every authority mutation is represented by an anchored local intent before dispatch, an anchored dispatch-release marker, and then a retained remote fact, rejection, or unresolved possibility. Positive local permission is applied only after a separate owner/epoch/finality/liveness recheck. Safety inventory is projected from these records plus bindings; it never infers safe emptiness from missing local permission. Recovery uses purpose-bound `SAFETY_RECONCILIATION` or `HISTORICAL_EVIDENCE` receipts for exact run/claim/authority/acquisition targets and cannot grant, renew, admit business work, sign acceptance, or create credit.

Evaluation and attestation use one canonical provenance manifest derived from opaque enrolled contexts and sealed source records. The manifest binds evidence references, validators, parents, scope, time and purpose for role/build/launch, clock, Human GO, environment, acquisition, custody/seal, evaluator and writer ancestry. Missing, duplicate, cyclic or mixed-lineage nodes are unverified; any TEST_ONLY acquisition or role ancestry permanently taints the artifact. `source_mode` and serialized verification labels do not create trust.

Real deployment must provision an authenticated HTTPS v3 issuer, immutable accepted artifacts, a protected launcher that observes and enforces the actual spawn before issuing role sessions, isolated credentials and signing keys, and a narrow independently valid recovery authority. Rehearsal must demonstrate those guarantees. This repository provisions none of them, and operational readiness remains blocked.
